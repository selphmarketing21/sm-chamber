What membership gets your business

Our members get connections, customers, and a stronger voice in the community.

Be found by neighbors

Your business listing appears in a searchable directory that customers use to find local help.

Meet people who buy local

Monthly mixers, ribbon cuttings, and a yearly gala put you in the room with other owners.

Get seen all year

Sponsor placements rotate fairly across the site, so every tier gets real exposure.

Seven reasons members stay

  1. 1

    Connections

    The owners you meet here become customers, partners, and friends.

  2. 2

    Credibility

    Our name on your door tells shoppers that you are invested here.

  3. 3

    Resources

    Get answers from peers who have faced the same problems you have.

  4. 4

    A voice

    We follow local and state issues and speak up for business at city hall.

  5. 5

    Visibility

    Ribbon cuttings, sponsorships, and events put your name in front of the public.

  6. 6

    Giving back

    Scholarships and service projects strengthen the town your business depends on.

  7. 7

    Business intelligence

    Stay informed about local issues, regional opportunities, and changes that may affect your business.
